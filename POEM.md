# Milestones

Before the first commit, a blank field waits.
A client names a thing that is not here.
Across a time zone, someone reads the brief
and leaves a question sharper than the plan.

A milestone holds a promise small enough
to test: a page that opens, a saved reply.
The diff turns green; a comment catches what
the passing checks were never taught to see.

Between the estimate and the invoice lies
the patient work of making words agree:
this is the scope, this is the change we chose,
this is the part that finally feels complete.

The task is closed. The payment finds its way.
A review leaves a trace of how it went.
FreelanceFlow keeps the record; somewhere else,
a person opens what another made.
