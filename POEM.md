# The Watch of the Digital Bastion

In labyrinths of compiled byte and thread,
Where silent daemons weave their midnight loom,
The unseen flaws through memory buffers spread,
And cast their shadows in the stack frame's room.

A yellow flare ignites the frozen screen,
The peel of abstraction stripped away to bone,
Where edge cases in dark subroutines convene,
And race conditions claim their fleeting throne.

With careful craft, the hunter steps in stride,
Through fuzzing sweeps and symbolic domains,
To turn the exploits that the codebase hid
Into the shield that unbroken remains.

For every patch that seals a leaky gate,
And every boundary verified anew,
Transforms the chaos of impending fate
Into the logic of the tested true.

So let the systems pulse without despair,
Across the wires where the data flows,
The Banana guards the cybernetic air,
A bastion safe from all malicious blows.
