# A Task With Three Doors

At the first door, a client names a need.
The page makes room for details, not a guess.
A proposal crosses from a face to fields;
the brief grows clearer where its edges meet.

At the second door, the routes ask what is meant.
A schema keeps a number from a name.
A message finds the work it belongs beside;
a role admits a hand, but not the whole house.

At the third door, the database remembers:
a job, a change, the person who agreed.
The screen may close; the record stays to answer
which promise moved, and which is waiting still.

FreelanceFlow, keep these three doors in view:
the words we show, the checks, the rows beneath.
Let every handoff carry what was asked;
let trust have somewhere solid it can stand.
