# The Sentinel's Ledger

A quiet script wakes before the sun,
its ledger open, columns bare and wide.
It walks the walls that other hands have spun,
and checks each lock the builders set aside.

It does not swing a sword or shout alarms,
but reads the seams where clever fingers pry.
It weighs the small neglects, the folded charms,
and logs the truth that careless eyes pass by.

When morning comes the hunters read the page,
each line a trail, each warning left in place.
The sentinel steps back and leaves the stage—
a patient guard that never shows its face.
