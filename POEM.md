# The Quiet Routing Table

At midnight, servers keep their patient light,  
A million small decisions cross the wire;  
Each packet finds a shape within the night,  
Each waiting thread remembers its desire.  

No thunder marks the mutex turning free,  
No bell announces queues returning calm;  
Yet careful hands make room for all to be,  
And turn contention into something warm.  

The system is not only steel and speed,  
Nor graphs that rise beneath a dashboard's glow;  
It is the promise that a signal, freed,  
Will reach the place it has been asked to go.  

So let the hidden engines hold their course,  
Let failures teach the routes to bend, not break;  
Good software is a quiet, faithful force—  
A bridge that stays awake for others' sake.
