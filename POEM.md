# The Code's Whisper

In silicon dreams where circuits breathe,  
A silent gardener tends to leaves  
That grow not earth, but logic sown—  
The seed of every line was known.  

Each loop a river, deep and vast,  
From algorithm's ancient past,  
We build our castles, stone on stone,  
In languages that have never been.  

So craft your verse with careful hand,  
Let bug and feature follow land,  
For in the GitHub ocean's tide,  
New worlds are waiting to abide.  

The pull request becomes a bridge,  
Of knowledge gathered, not a ridge,  
But valley floor where contributors meet—  
A bounty earned, and conscience sweet.
