# FreelanceFlow

*A poem for the FreelanceFlow monorepo*

## I. The Brief

The brief goes out at half past nine —
a budget range, a scope, a date.
Somewhere a stranger reads the line
and answers, though the hour is late.

## II. The Proposal

She counts the work in honest hours
and sets the bid where value lives;
she sends no rain of borrowed powers,
only the proof of what she gives.

## III. The Wire

Between them runs a quiet wire —
the API's patient, ordered spine,
a Stripe-shaped lock, a review's fire,
a dashboard drawn in clean design.

## IV. The Work

Then messages and milestones flow,
the pull request, the merge, the test;
the `OPEN` status, row by row,
turns green, and briefly, all is rest.

## V. The Ledger

The payment clears; the ledger writes
a settled number, small and true;
a five-star review arrives, and lights
the dashboard where the work shows through.

## VI. Coda

So FreelanceFlow — the brief, the bid,
the wire, the merge, the paid invoice —
is just a place where strangers did
good work, and both sides had a choice.

---

**One-line note on the creative decision:** I wrote it as six short quatrains tracing a single contract from brief to paid review, because the repository is literally a pipeline of those stages — the poem's structure mirrors `Job → Proposal → Payment → Review` in `packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma`.
