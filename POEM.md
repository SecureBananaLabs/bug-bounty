# The Bug Hunter's Code

*For SecureBananaLabs — where every vulnerability tells a story*

---

## I. The Open Port

At midnight in the stack trace,
Where null pointers dance with grace,
A buffer waits, patient, vast—
One byte over, and the castle falls.

We read the logs like ancient texts,
Each exception a paradox:
The code that works by accident
Is code that breaks by design.

## II. The Patch

Twelve cups of coffee deep,
The commit message reads "*hotfix*"—
But we know the truth:
Every fix births two bugs,

A conservation law of chaos,
Entropy in syntax sugar,
The compiler hums its warning,
We push to prod anyway.

## III. The Bounty

So here we stand, line after line,
Trading sleep for ship conditions,
Not for glory (that fades fast),
Not for fame (the repo stars),

But for the moment when the scanner
Goes quiet at last—
No red flags, no criticals,
Just green tests, passing.

And someone, somewhere, clicks *Merge*
on work that kept the data safe.
That is the bounty worth hunting.

---

*Form: Free verse with loose ABAB rhyme in stanzas I & III*
*Theme: The daily reality of security engineers — not the Hollywood hacking, but the patience, the coffee, the 2am rollbacks, and the quiet satisfaction of a merged patch.*
*Tone: Earnest but wry — respects the craft while acknowledging its absurdities.*

**Creative decision:** I chose free verse over strict form because bug hunting itself isn't elegant—it's messy, iterative, and real. The poem mirrors that: structure emerges from chaos, just like a good patch emerges from debugging.
