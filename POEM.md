# The Sentinel in the Wire

Beneath the canopy of routed streams,
Where Express listens to unmarshalled dreams,
A golden peel protects the stateless core,
From malformed payloads knocking at the door.
The Zod boundaries tighten around the frame,
Asserting truth to every field and name.

Through asynchronous corridors of light,
The token signs its secret in the night;
A Bearer header bearing trust and role,
Connecting client to the worker’s soul.
From job proposals inked in clean design,
To escrowed payments minted on the line.

No rogue injection passes through the gate,
Where strict schemas arbitrate each state;
No unbounded buffer breaks the heap,
While in-memory ledgers vigil keep.
The Banana sentinel stands tall and clear,
A beacon shining through the frontier.

From SQLite vaults to micro-payment feeds,
Automated audits answering real needs;
The code compiles, deterministic, pure,
With every unit assertion made secure.
Green suites ignite across the terminal screen,
The cleanest architecture ever seen.

So let the concurrent callers freely flow,
Through hardened gateways where the watchers glow.
A decentralized craft of steel and grace,
Securing every corner of the space.
