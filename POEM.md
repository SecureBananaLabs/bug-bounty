# Signal and Noise

*For bug-bounty — an original poem*

---

## I. The Scan

The scanner moves through open ports  
the way a hand moves through tall grass —  
feeling for the thing that doesn't fit,  
the absence where a wall should pass.

---

## II. The Finding

In every system, somewhere, a gap:  
a door left open, trust misplaced,  
an input never sanitized,  
a token never properly erased.

The bug is not a flaw of code alone —  
it lives between intent and execution,  
the space where someone wrote *secure enough*  
and left the rest as future resolution.

---

## III. The Report

So here it is: the numbered line,  
the proof-of-concept, the CVE.  
Not written in anger, not in pride —  
just evidence of what should not be.

The patch will come. The score will drop.  
The system grows a little more complete.  
This is the work: to find, to name,  
to hand the keys back, clean and neat.

---

*Creative decision: I chose a three-stanza arc — scan, discovery, disclosure —  
mirroring the lifecycle of responsible security research. The tone is  
matter-of-fact rather than dramatic: good security work is methodical,  
not heroic.*
