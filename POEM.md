# Symphony of the Freelance Flow

### I. The Monorepo Tapestry
From `packages/db` the schemas rise,
Where Prisma maps our relational skies.
Users and proposals in declarative grace,
Bound to PostgreSQL in a structured embrace.
A monorepo born of TypeScript and light,
Where clients and builders commune in the night.

### II. The Frontend Canvas
In `apps/web` where the App Router breathes,
Next.js fourteen weaves reactive wreaths.
From job board cards to dashboard streams,
Freelancers craft their modular dreams.
Dynamic routes render with hydration profound,
Where talent and vision are seamlessly found.

### III. The Express Core
Through `apps/api` the middleware glides,
Where rate limits govern and security presides.
Zod guards the gates against malformed deceit,
While controllers and services orchestrate each feat.
With JWTs minted and Stripe webhooks clear,
The flow of creation knows neither doubt nor fear.

### IV. The Living Marketplace
A handshake of tokens, a milestone unlocked,
A review of excellence respectfully docked.
No fragmented silos, no friction in store,
Just pure execution from core unto core.
Here talent thrives free in each commit and row,
Behold the architecture of FreelanceFlow.
