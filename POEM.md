# The Fortress of the Golden Peel

In headers deep where origins collide,
A silent sentry guards the open port;
No rogue request may cross the strict divide,
Nor forged token breach the ciphered court.

Through ninety-ninth percentiles, swift and clean,
Each packet races down the fibered stream;
Zero-trust gates inspect the unseen scene,
While deterministic checks uphold the dream.

When fuzzed payloads knock at midnight's door,
And race conditions test the mutex lock,
The banana fortress stands forevermore—
Immutable, resilient as the rock.
