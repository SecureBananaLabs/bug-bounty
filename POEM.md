# The Coder's Quest

In circuits deep where logic flows,  
A billion pathways light and glow,  
We trace the bugs where darkness grows,  
And watch our digital gardens grow.

With hands of code and eyes so keen,  
We build the future, byte by byte,  
Through tangled lines and screens unseen,  
We bring the chaos into light.

So let us write with steady hand,  
The future's code, the future's plan,  
Across this digital and promised land,  
We are the builders, woman, man.
