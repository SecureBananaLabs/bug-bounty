# The Scan

In quiet hours before dawn breaks through,
the researcher types a single query—
ports, paths, parameters, all askew,
the application's truth a mystery.
Each request a probe, each response a clue,
the attack surface mapped in increments,
no exploit yet, just understanding grown,
the patient cartography of what's known.

## The Finding

Then something shifts—a parameter replies
with data it should never have returned.
The heartbeat skips. The cursor rests. The eyes
grow wide. A validation left unlearned,
a boundary trusts what it should despise,
and suddenly the system's seams are shown.
Not panic, not excitement—just the weight
of knowing what the code could not anticipate.

## The Report

The finding written plainly, step by step,
reproduction clear, impact assessed with care.
No drama, just the contract that we keep:
the ninety days to fix before we share.
The vendor told, the patch deployed, the debt
of silence paid in full. The CVE filed clean.
The researcher signs off, the cycle closed,
another gap found, fixed, and then disclosed.

> Creative note: The poem follows the three-stage security disclosure lifecycle (scan → finding → report) in terza rima-inspired stanzas. The tone is deliberately restrained—reflecting the professional discipline of responsible disclosure rather than the sensationalism often associated with "hacking."
