# The Watch

*A poem for SecureBananaLabs/bug-bounty*

---

In the silence of the trace log's stream,
where registers hold their quiet dream,
I set my breakpoint on the edge—
the place where trust becomes a pledge.

The stack grows tall like ancient bone,
each frame a world, a fleeting home.
I step through calls with patient care,
through functions worn by time and wear.

A buffer waits, a gaping door,
where input flows and bounds no more.
One byte too many, softly placed,
and memory's mask is gently chased.

What secrets hide in heap and stack?
What paths lead forward, which lead back?
The debugger reveals its art—
not breaking things, but knowing heart.

---

*Written for the hunt—the patient craft of finding what was never meant to be found.*
