# The Monorepo of FreelanceFlow

In nested folders where the modules dwell,
The Next.js router rings its hydrate bell.
From `apps/web` the render passes down,
A server component wearing clean layout crown.
Tailwind utilities align the frame,
While client hooks declare the freelancer's name.

Beneath the glass, where Node and Express reign,
The middleware unwraps the bearer chain.
A JWT verified before the route may fire,
To fetch the bids the client hearts desire.
From `packages/db` the Prisma clients call,
Guarding the types that keep our queries tall.

Atomic migrations seal the escrow tight,
As Stripe events dispatch into the night.
No race conditions, no unhandled trace,
Just clean concurrency in microspace.
Through lint and benchmarks under pressure proven,
A decentralized marketplace smoothly woven.
