# The Quiet Sentinel

In silent threads where cycles keep,
While unobserved the watchers sleep,
A single branch, an untracked state,
Decides the system's final fate.

No flourish marks the hardened line,
No rhetoric in strict design;
The invariant that holds the key
Declares what must forever be.

Through bounded steps and measured grace,
No leaked intent shall leave a trace;
For truth within the code resides
Where reason reigns and quiet guides.

Across the wire the tokens fly,
Beneath an uncorrupted sky;
The work complete, the ledger clear,
The sentinel remains sincere.
