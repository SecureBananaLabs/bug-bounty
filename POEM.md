# FreelanceFlow

*An original poem written for this project.*

## I. The Brief

A client signs in, tired and late,
With work too large to bear alone;
They post a job, they set a rate,
And cast a hopeful seed unknown.

## II. The Bid

Across the world a freelancer wakes,
Reads the listing, drafts a plan;
A proposal forms, a small handshake,
A skill made visible, as only craft can.

## III. The Build

Then TypeScript hums on either side —
Next.js pages, Express routes,
Prisma models opening wide
The doors through which the work flows out.

## IV. The Flow

No office wall, no crowded hall,
Just dashboards, messages, and trust;
The marketplace becomes a call
That turns a skill to something just.

---

*Creative note: I chose a four-stanza ABAB rhyme scheme to mirror the four-step
arc this platform automates — brief, bid, build, and flow — and kept the tone
warm but workmanlike, like the product itself.*
