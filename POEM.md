# Quiet Before the Find

Between the clone and the first commit,
while the vault settles into its own hum,
I read the code the way you read weather —
not for what it says, but for the pressure dropping.

Stanza two was never going to be the bug.
The bug has no manners; it arrives dressed as a comment,
a `// TODO` from a Tuesday two years gone,
a rounding error that only confesses under load.
I keep a lantern anyway. I keep a lantern anyway.

The tests pass. The tests always pass.
They are polite, and they were written by the same hand
that wrote the flaw, so they nod and step aside.
What I want is the small refusal —
the endpoint that answers one beat late,
the fee that doubles when nobody is watching.

Stanza four: morning, coffee gone bitter,
a `p99` like a held breath, and there —
a curve in the logic where a stranger could stand
without a ticket. I write it down gently,
the way you write down a name you'll say for years.

Bounty is not the word. Receipt is not the word.
The word is the quiet before the find,
the lantern, the lantern, the lantern,
and finally: the door that opens the way it should.
