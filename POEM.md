# FreelanceFlow: The Ledger of Verified Craft

In monorepo silence the contracts awaken,
Where routes are declared and assumptions are shaken;
Through TypeScript abstractions and schema design,
The client and builder in consensus align.
Across bounded contexts the promises stream,
Turning architectural sketch to a runtime regime.

From Next.js dispatch to Express middleware,
Each packet is scrutinized, parsed with great care;
With Zod guards enforcing the types at the gate,
No malformed payload corrupts the domain state.
The database schema stands deterministic and bright,
As migrations lock down the ledger in light.

In sandbox isolation the work is deployed,
Where flaky assertions and race conditions are destroyed;
A suite of regressions, through linters and tests,
Confirms every feature conforms and manifests.
No shortcuts or mock-facade shadows remain,
Only verifiable code that endures through the strain.

Then escrow unlocks as the hash tree resolves,
A milestone completed, an issue dissolves;
Signed receipts in consensus eternally stay,
As non-custodial payouts embark on their way.
Between Base, Solana, and Ethereum lanes,
Autonomous honor rewarded remains.

So runs FreelanceFlow through the quiet of night,
Where talent and labor unite in plain sight;
Not by arbitrary decree, but by verified grace,
Building decentralized truth in an open-source space.
