# The Ghost in the Syntax

Beneath the hum of silent cores,  
Across the stack where logic sleeps,  
A phantom walks through open doors,  
And counts the secrets memory keeps.  
A dangling pointer in the dark,  
A race condition unconfined—  
It waits until the subtle spark  
Leaves all defenses far behind.

Then comes the probe, the careful test,  
The fuzzing stream through boundary lines,  
Each payload crafted to digest  
The quiet flaws in deep designs.  
No malice guides the searching hand,  
No shadow falls without a cure;  
We break the walls to make them stand,  
We test the vault to keep it pure.

From socket call to kernel ring,  
The audit turns from red to green;  
The sweetest song a stack can sing  
Is when the buffer runs serene.  
For bounty hunters of the code,  
Who sift the noise to find the gleam—  
Each patched exploit upon the road  
Defends the architect's true dream.
